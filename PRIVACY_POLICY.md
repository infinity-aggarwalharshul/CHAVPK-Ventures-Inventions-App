# Privacy Policy

## Overview

This project may process personal information in relation to account creation, service access, user support, and platform usage. The purpose of this policy is to provide clear information about how personal information is handled.

## Information we may collect

Depending on the implementation, the platform may collect:

- name and display name
- email address
- phone number
- company or institution details
- address, city, state, or pin code where relevant
- account credentials and authentication metadata
- usage data necessary for functional operation and support

## Why information is collected

Personal data may be processed for:

- account registration and authentication
- service delivery and support
- platform security and fraud prevention
- compliance and legal obligations
- product improvement and operational analytics

## Data minimization

The platform should collect only the minimum data required for the purpose stated. Unnecessary personal information should not be retained.

## Storage and security

Data should be protected by:

- secure access control
- encrypted transmission where applicable
- restricted internal access
- secure cloud configuration and monitoring
- periodic review of retention and deletion rules

## User rights

Applicable law may give users rights to:

- access their data
- request correction
- request deletion where lawful
- know the purpose for collection
- object to certain processing where applicable

## Cookies and tracking

If analytics or marketing tools are introduced, the site should provide clear notice, consent controls, and compliance review before deployment.

## Third-party services

This repository may integrate with external systems such as Firebase or Gemini APIs. Those systems may have separate privacy practices. In production, those integrations should be reviewed under the project’s privacy and compliance framework.

## Retention

Data retention should follow a documented retention schedule. Personal data should be removed when no longer necessary for the business purpose or legal requirement.

## Legal basis

In a production environment, the legal basis for processing should be documented, including consent, contractual necessity, legal obligation, or legitimate interest where applicable.

## Contact

For privacy concerns, contact:

- support@chavpk.in

This policy should be reviewed with qualified legal counsel before any public or commercial launch in India.
